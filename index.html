Code Calculator - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Code Calculator ("the app") is published by TapyrLabs ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: it collects none.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information.
  • It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.
  • It does not make network connections of its own.
  • It does not read your documents, clipboard, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

What the app stores on your device

Everything below stays on your PC and is never sent to us:

  • Settings: your chosen show/hide hotkey.
  • Trial status: the date you started the free Pro trial, so the app can tell when the 2 months are over.
  • License key (website edition only): the license key you entered, so Pro stays unlocked.

Uninstalling the app removes the Microsoft Store edition's data. For the website edition, the uninstaller removes the program; you can delete %LocalAppData%\TapyrLabs\CodeCalculator to remove the stored license/trial files.

Purchases

If you buy Pro in the Microsoft Store edition, the purchase is handled entirely by Microsoft under Microsoft's own terms and privacy statement. We receive no payment details; the app only asks the Microsoft Store whether your account owns Pro. If you buy a license key from our website, the payment provider processes the purchase and sends you the key; their privacy policy applies to that transaction.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
